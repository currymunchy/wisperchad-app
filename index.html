What it does

Dictate anywhere

Hold Ctrl+Win on Windows or Ctrl+Option+D on Mac, talk, and let go. Clean text, with the "ums" taken out, appears wherever your cursor is.

Write replies for you

Hold Ctrl+Win+Alt or Ctrl+Option+R and say what you mean, however rough. wisperChad reads the message on your screen and drafts a reply. You read it and press Send yourself.

Private by design

Your voice and your messages never leave your computer. Speech and writing models run locally, with no account and nothing sent to the cloud.

Download

wisperChad is free. Get the installer for your computer from the latest release, and setup takes care of the rest.

Windows 10 and 11

  1. Download wisperChad_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe from the latest release.
  2. Open it. Windows may say the publisher is unknown, because the app isn't code-signed yet: click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Setup checks your computer, downloads the right models and has you try it out.

Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer)

  1. Download wisperChad_x.y.z_aarch64.dmg from the latest release, and drag wisperChad into Applications. Needs macOS 13.3 or newer.
  2. The first time, macOS blocks it because it isn't notarized yet. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. If macOS says the app is damaged, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/wisperChad.app in Terminal.
  3. Allow Microphone, Accessibility (to type for you) and Screen Recording (to read the message you're replying to).

Dictation alone runs on most computers. Writing replies works best with 6 GB of memory or more.

Privacy and terms

Your data. wisperChad doesn't collect, store or send anything you say or type. Audio, transcripts and the text it reads on your screen are processed on your computer only. The app goes online only to download the AI models you choose, from their publishers on Hugging Face, and its writing engine from GitHub. It has no accounts, analytics or ads.

Support. Coffees and sponsorships are optional and are handled by Buy Me a Coffee and GitHub Sponsors, with payments processed by Stripe. They don't buy a product or service. If you paid by mistake, contact me and I'll help sort it out.
