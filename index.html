What it does

Dictate anywhere

Hold Ctrl+Win, talk, and let go. Clean text, with the "ums" taken out, appears wherever your cursor is: email, chat, documents or any app.

Write replies for you

Hold Ctrl+Win+Alt and say what you mean, however rough. wisperChad reads the message on your screen and drafts a polite reply. You read it and press Send yourself.

Private by design

Your voice and your messages never leave your computer. Speech and writing models run locally, with no account and nothing sent to the cloud.

Made for your computer

Setup checks your PC and picks models that fit, from fast speech models like Parakeet to writing models like Qwen3.5. Older PCs get a dictation-only option.

Download

wisperChad is free. Download the latest version, open the installer, and setup takes care of the rest.

  1. Download wisperChad_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe from the latest release.
  2. Open it. Windows may say the publisher is unknown, because the app isn't code-signed yet: click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. wisperChad checks your computer, recommends models, downloads them and has you try it out.

Needs Windows 10 or 11. Dictation alone runs on most PCs. Writing replies works best with 6 GB of RAM or more. A Mac version is in testing.

Privacy and terms

Your data. wisperChad doesn't collect, store or send anything you say or type. Audio, transcripts and the text it reads on your screen are processed on your computer only. The app goes online only to download the AI models you choose, from their publishers on Hugging Face, and its writing engine from GitHub. It has no accounts, analytics or ads.

Sponsorships. Sponsorships are optional and are handled by GitHub Sponsors, with payments processed by Stripe. They don't buy a product or service. If you sponsored by mistake, contact me and I'll help sort it out with GitHub.
